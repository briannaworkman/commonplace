[00:01:49] **MARISOL:** It's a funnel, but the important thing is it's a funnel *over time*.

forgot wasn't load-bearing.

[00:02:08] **MARISOL:** Forgetting is the filter. If you come back in three weeks and

see what's left.

[00:02:26] **MARISOL:** It's a huge relief! Because the alternative is you sitting there
